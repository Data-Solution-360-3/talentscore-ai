How it works

From application to a scored interview, in one link.

The same flow runs for every applicant, so everyone is measured the same way.

Step one

Screen the CV

Read against your rubric — scored twice, blended, recomputed in code so the number is reproducible.

Step two

Interview live

Qualifying applicants flow into a short live AI interview through the same link.

Step three

Review the shortlist

One overall score combines CV and interview, with the evidence behind each dimension.

The live interview

Half the product is a conversation.

The CV gets a candidate in the door. The interview is where they show how they think — the same short, structured conversation for everyone who qualifies.

1.

Create the job

Pick a role category — it sets the scoring rubric and the interview's flavor.

2.

Generate the questions

One set per job, drafted once and approved by you before anything goes live — the same questions for every candidate.

3.

A live spoken conversation

Qualifying applicants talk with an AI interviewer that asks your approved questions — English by default, Bangla in beta — each one also shown as on-screen text.

4.

Scored with evidence

Every dimension cites a quote from the answer, interview and CV combine into one overall, and a human makes the final call.

5.

Monitored, honestly

Camera, screen, and tab signals are recorded and anomalies are flagged for a human to review — a signal, never an automatic decision.

The rubric

Six dimensions, weighted your way.

Balanced by default; tune per role, or start from a preset.

25%

Skills match

Matched by what tools imply — not keyword overlap.

20%

Experience relevance

How closely real work maps to the role.

20%

Achievement & impact

Evidence of quantified results.

15%

Role alignment

Seniority, scope, and fit.

10%

Education & certs

Weighted by relevance.

10%

Stability & tenure

Surfaced for review, never auto-reject.
