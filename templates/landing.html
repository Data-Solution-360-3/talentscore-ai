Questions

Straight answers

What is the live AI interview?
A real-time spoken conversation in the browser — your job's questions, identical for every candidate, every question also shown as text. Some are answered in writing.
Is scoring fair to candidates whose English isn't fluent?
The scorers grade substance, not fluency — a sharp answer in broken English must outscore a fluent empty one, verified with test transcripts before every scoring change.
Does the proctoring catch cheating?
It produces signals for human review — monitoring, flagged snapshots, a coverage timeline. Nothing is auto-rejected, and no browser system is cheat-proof.
Does a missing requirement reject a candidate?
No. It applies a capped penalty and pushes the candidate down the ranking — never off it.
What happens when the two scoring passes disagree?
More than 12 points apart: flagged low-confidence and downgraded to Maybe. Never upgraded.
Where does candidate data go?
CV text and transcripts go to OpenAI's API for scoring — we say so plainly. PDFs delete after 30 days, and you can delete any screening.
